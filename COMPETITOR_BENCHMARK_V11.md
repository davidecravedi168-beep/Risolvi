# RISOLVI V11 — competitor benchmark and independent innovation

This benchmark studies public product patterns only. RISOLVI does not copy proprietary code, assets, datasets, or wording.

## Resolver (UK)
Public pattern: company-first complaint routing, personal case file, evidence, progress tracking, rights guidance, escalation to ombudsmen/regulators.

RISOLVI implementation: Resolution Command adds an explicit escalation ladder, next-best-action, case prioritisation and an exportable Resolution Pack. Unlike a static directory, escalation is fail-closed when case readiness is too low.

## AirHelp (global travel)
Public pattern: proactive monitoring, eligibility alerts, one-place case tracking, fast action when a disruption occurs.

RISOLVI implementation: operational follow-up clock surfaces cases that have gone quiet for 7/14 days and pushes the next action into the case cockpit. It does not infer legal deadlines from silence.

## FairShake / consumer arbitration platforms (US)
Public pattern: guided escalation from complaint to formal dispute/arbitration, document preparation, structured case handling.

RISOLVI implementation: sector-specific escalation maps, evidence checklists and human-review gates. Formal escalation is not presented as automatic legal advice.

## DoNotPay (historical self-help model)
Public pattern: broad action catalogue for subscriptions, refunds, fees, bureaucracy and consumer self-help.

Safety lesson: RISOLVI must never present itself as a lawyer or claim equivalence to professional legal advice without substantiation. Human handoff and source verification remain first-class controls.

## V11 innovations
- Resolution Command: next action + readiness + follow-up + recorded-deadline status in one cockpit.
- Escalation Lock: advanced escalation is blocked below 60% readiness.
- Recorded Deadline Guard: displays a deadline only if one is actually stored; otherwise states that the legal term must be verified.
- Human Handoff Gate: ambiguous/generic/low-readiness cases surface review instead of pretending certainty.
- Resolution Pack: one-click privacy-conscious export of structured case facts, evidence list, steps and escalation path.
- Sector depth: improved evidence and escalation handling for energy and telecom cases.

## Product principle
Automate organisation and procedure; fail closed on legal certainty. Readiness is preparation quality, not probability of winning.
